Privacy Policy — Meteor Pilot

Developer: Biscuit Paw · Effective date: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Meteor Pilot (package com.biscuitpaw.meteorpilot, the “App”) published by Biscuit Paw (“we”, “us”) handles information. The App does not require an account, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, location or any other information that identifies you directly.

1. Information stored on your device

The App may save your game progress (for example, levels reached, coins and plane customization) and settings on your device. This data is not sent to us and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The App uses the third-party services listed below. These services may automatically collect information from your device and process it under their own privacy policies. We do not receive information from them that identifies you personally.

3. How the information is used

  • to show ads, including personalized ads if you have allowed them;
  • to limit how often ads are shown and to prevent ad fraud.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for any purpose other than those listed above.

4. Your choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland. Before personalized ads are shown, the App asks for your consent using the Google User Messaging Platform. If you decline, you may still see non-personalized ads.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • You can stop all data collection by the App by uninstalling it.

5. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the App. Information collected by third-party services is kept according to their policies. Because we do not know who you are, we usually cannot link this data to you, but you can contact us and we will help you make a request to the relevant provider.

6. Data security

Data transmitted by the third-party services listed above is encrypted in transit.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add analytics or in-app purchases. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at biscuitpawstudio@gmail.com.
